# Pacing and story rules

## Measured numbers (17 kallawaymarketing reels)
- Scene length: median 1.45 s, mean 2.1 s. 24% of scenes are under 0.5 s, 40% under 1 s, 15% over 4 s (and those
  long ones build inside).
- About 4 cuts in the first 5 s (hooks with a word-by-word build get 10 to 18).
- Speech about 3.8 words per second. Reels are 30 to 135 s; most are 60 to 110 s.
- Screen time: 72% SPLIT, 28% FULL.

## The reel skeleton
| part | time | scene types |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 3 s | proof grid (1), word-by-word hook (2) or kinetic opener (3). Fastest cuts of the video. |
| Name reveal | 3 to 6 s | FULL with the serif term (4), then the tracker's first appearance (5a to 5f). |
| Steps | the body | per step: tracker shows the step, then 2 to 6 explanation scenes (6 to 19), then a FULL beat. |
| Recap | 5 to 10 s | the tracker or hero object, whole, with all items lit; or an orbit (13). |
| CTA | last 5 to 12 s | CTA card (22), then FULL with the stacked keyword caption. |

## Choosing a scene for a script line (decision order)
1. Line names a step or section: tracker (5).
2. Line is about scripts, writing, structure: script doc (6).
3. Line points to a real example or app: device (7) or annotated example (8).
4. Line compares old vs new: before / after (9).
5. Line has a number, speed, more or less: graph / gauge (10) or big-number caption.
6. Line names a tool or product: UI recreation (11) or pipeline (12).
7. Line says "connects", "everyone", "top creators": orbit (13).
8. Line is about viewers or buyers: audience icons (14). About the mind: brain (15).
9. Line tells a turn, a history, a choice: line / path (16).
10. Line uses a metaphor: draw the metaphor (17).
11. Line lists 3+ things fast: carousel (18).
12. Line is emotional, an opinion, a transition, or a "snap back" ("But here's the thing"): FULL beat (21).
13. Line describes a vivid real thing: B-roll burst (20).

## Rhythm
- A FULL beat after every 2 to 6 graphic scenes. Put it on the word with the most feeling.
- Lists and rapid examples: one visual per word (0.2 to 0.5 s).
- Explanations: one visual per phrase (1 to 3 s), building inside.
- Hold longer (4 to 8 s) only when the visual keeps building (script doc, mind map, chart drawing).
- The tracker returns before every step, even if it was on screen 20 s ago.

## Captions
- One word at a time (the word being spoken), sometimes two short words together ("the world", "I'll send you").
- Swap on each word start. No karaoke colour, no pop animation.
- Framework name and CTA keyword switch to the serif italic.
- On FULL beats the caption is the same single-word style.
