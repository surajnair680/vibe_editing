# Scene types (the 4-column table)

Built from 17 kallawaymarketing reels (807 detected scenes, Nov 2026). Reel numbers refer to
the study (kept on the user's Mac in `edit_like_kallaway/analysis/kallawaymarketing/`, not in this repo) (01 Triple hook, 02 Dopamine loop, 03 Application content, 04 6x5 plan,
05 Script format, 07 Story ladder, 08 / 12 / 18 Claude social media, 09 Illusion of novelty, 10 Desire hook,
11 Reference database, 14 Comprehension maxing, 15 Dopamine ladder, 16 High effort era, 17 Triple loop, 20 AI tools).
Example frames for each type: `references/frames/<nn>-<type>/`.

Every scene does one storytelling job. If you can't name the job, cut the scene.

| # | Type of scene | Design | Animation | When it's used (and why) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Proof-grid hook** | Light ground. Two-line title (line 2 often in the red box). 2x3 or 3x3 grid of real reel thumbnails, rounded 12 px, each with a small view-count pill under it (eye icon + "4.1M"; green pill in 03, black in 11). | Thumbnails pop in with a short stagger (0.05 s), slight scale 0.92 to 1. Title is already on screen at frame 0. | First 0 to 3 s when the claim is "this works". Proof before the promise, so the viewer trusts the next 60 s. (03, 07, 15) |
| 2 | **Word-by-word title hook** | Title builds one word at a time in the top third. Under it, a new example card / board state for every word. | Hard cut every 0.2 to 0.4 s, on each spoken word, for the first 1 to 2.5 s. Words fade + slide up 12 px, or blur-in. | Hooks that name a big outcome ("How do you make boring content addictive?"). The per-word change is the "visual stun gun": motion in the first second stops the scroll. (02, 09, 10, 11, 16) |
| 3 | **Kinetic type opener** | Mixed sizes in one block: small words, one HUGE heavy word, grey trailing word ("Here's how to / PLAN / your next"). Big number + stacked small words ("30 days of content"). | Each word lands on its spoken word. The block then turns into the next object (calendar, grid) and flies off with motion blur. One continuous canvas, no cut. | When the hook is a plan or a number. The size contrast tells the viewer the one word that matters. (04) |
| 4 | **Name reveal (A-roll)** | Full-screen talking head. Caption switches to the italic serif, slightly larger, and holds the whole term ("Comprehension Maxing.", "story ladder."). | Term builds word by word, then holds 0.8 to 1.5 s. | The moment the framework gets its name, at 2 to 5 s. Naming it makes it feel like a real, ownable method. (03, 07, 09, 14, 15, 17) |
| 5a | **Chapter tracker: pill list** | Title "The *Desire Hook* Method" + 3 to 5 dark rounded rows (fill #111, 1 px border), number on the left, thin red left tab. Upcoming rows are BLURRED (8 px) and dim. | Returns before every step: the current row sharpens, gets the red tab and white text, scale 1.04; done rows stay sharp but grey. | Every "Step one / two / three". Shows progress, which keeps people for the next step. (01, 10, 16) |
| 5b | **Chapter tracker: hero object** | One glossy 3D object that IS the framework: red neon ladder (6 rungs), red infinity loop with numbers 1 to 4. Labels on the object. | Camera flies along the object to the current label (0.2 s whip steps, motion blur), zooms in, then pulls out to the whole object at the recap. Labels type on letter by letter. | Frameworks with levels or a cycle. The object is the memory hook: viewers remember "the ladder". (02, 07, 15) |
| 5c | **Chapter tracker: hub and spokes** | Red circle node on the left ("Ultimate Storytelling"), curved thin lines to 4 dark pills with icons. Unreached pills blurred. | Next spoke lights per step. Final recap: the hub sits inside a big radar ring with a red glow. | 4-part frameworks. Shows the parts belong to one system. (09) |
| 5d | **Chapter tracker: roadmap arc** | A big curved line on the left edge with a dot. Huge black italic number "1" + two-line label ("Find / *Winning Topics*"). Light notebook ground. | The camera travels along the arc to the next number. | Step-by-step plans on the light theme. (04) |
| 5e | **Chapter tracker: pinned TOC** | Tiny list of all sections pinned top-left; current one lit. Outlined section pill at top centre. | Stays for the whole listicle; the lit item moves down. | Long lists (8 tools). The viewer always knows how far is left. (20) |
| 5f | **Section badge** | Small glass icon tile (rounded square, icon) + label. | Starts big in the centre, shrinks to the top and stays as the header for the section. | Two-part tips ("Visual Alignment", "2"). (14) |
| 6 | **Script / document box** | Dark card with thin border (or red corner brackets). Header "Script" in italic serif. Grey placeholder lines; real lines in white. Colour code: red = hook, yellow = lock-in, magenta = body, white = closing, green = CTA, with small square bullets. | Lines draw in left to right one after another (0.04 s stagger). Highlighted lines get a colour wash. Each new step adds a colour (cumulative). | Anything about scripts, structure, or "what to write". The colours make an abstract structure visible. (01, 05, 07, 10, 17) |
| 7 | **Device showcase** | iPhone or tablet frame (black bezel, white 2 px rim, soft glow) with a real reel, feed or app UI inside. | Phone slides up or scales in. Content inside scrolls or plays. Whip transitions with motion blur between screens; the screen can flash a colour tint (green on "unskippable"). | Showing a real example, an app, or "this is what it looks like". Real UI = credibility. (01, 04, 08, 10, 11) |
| 8 | **Annotated example** | One vertical reel card (rounded 16 px, thin white border) with labels around it: dashed leader lines to small labels, red tag boxes in italic serif with a hand-drawn red curved arrow, neon highlight boxes around a number, speech-bubble pill + user icon. | Labels pop in on their spoken word. Arrows draw on (stroke animation 0.3 s). | Breaking down WHY an example works ("Colors / Motion / Brightness", "Wtf is going on"). (01, 02, 15) |
| 9 | **Before / after compare** | Two cards side by side with italic serif headers ("Old Advice" vs "New Reveal", "Info Hook" vs "Desire Hook"). The winner has red or green words. | Left card first, right card a beat later; or the same card swaps its text. | Contrasting the old way with the new way. Contrast is how the brain notices change. (09, 10) |
| 10 | **Graph / gauge** | Dark rounded card. Line chart with a red drop curve vs green curve; retention chart; speedometer gauge (green to red, needle, red button under it); semicircle "pendulum" gauge; heat-map screenshot. | Lines draw left to right; needle swings with overshoot; numbers count up. | Any claim about data, speed, or "more vs less". Turns a sentence into proof. (01, 09, 14, 16) |
| 11 | **Brand / UI recreation** | Clean rebuild of real UI: Claude prompt box, Instagram profile card with a red follower pill, skills list, dashboard. White rounded cards on the light theme. | Text types into the prompt box; follower pill counts up (11k to 44k); cursor clicks a button. | Tutorials and tool reels. Looks like the real product, so it feels doable. (08, 12, 18, 20) |
| 12 | **Element pipeline** | Logos (rounded squares, dotted outline ring) joined by thin white elbow lines or a chain-link icon; prompt tree (master doc to 3 child pills); many-to-one: a grid of thumbnails that collapses into one logo. | Lines draw from source to target; items fly along the line; grid items fly in and vanish into the logo with blur. | Automations, workflows, "plug A into B". Shows cause and effect. (08, 12, 18, 20) |
| 13 | **Orbit / circular lines** | Centre object (logo, brain, ladder, title) with a dashed ring; icons, avatars or reel thumbnails sit ON the ring or around it. | Ring draws on; items pop onto it in sequence; slow rotation. | "This connects everything", "top creators all do this", ecosystems. (07, 12, 14, 15, 18) |
| 14 | **Audience / user icons** | User glyphs: green dots around a centre user in a dashed radar circle; a red glowing circle with one user; red users gathering on a ring. | Dots fade in randomly; the red glow pulses. | Talking about viewers, buyers, "your audience". (01, 09, 17) |
| 15 | **Brain** | Realistic 3D glowing brain (white wireframe look, or red inside a silhouette head). Colour = state: red bad, yellow middle, green good. | Brain floats, glows, changes colour on the key word. | Viewer psychology: dopamine, comprehension, attention. (02, 09, 14) |
| 16 | **Line / path diagram** | Story arc: diagonal dashed line with a red dot, then a green segment "Contrarian Snap Back". Road that splits into A (dashed) and B (glowing curve) with a light dot. Horizontal timeline with nodes and thumbnails, orange progress line. Branch tree with outlined pill nodes and a red dot on the active node. | Dot travels along the path; the camera slides sideways along a timeline; branches grow. | Storytelling turns, history ("2021 to 2024 to 2026"), choices. (01, 02, 03, 16) |
| 17 | **Metaphor illustration** | The spoken image, drawn literally in one rounded card: fishing line over water, casino chips and cards, a magnet pulling a progress bar, a hawk diving at a red target, a turbo gauge. | Simple motion that acts out the metaphor (line yanks, chip flips, magnet pulls). | Whenever the script uses a metaphor. The picture makes the idea stick. (01, 02, 09, 14, 17) |
| 18 | **Listicle carousel** | Horizontal row of reel cards with labels above; or stacked dark pills with a red left tab ("Lazy / Copy Paste / Low Effort"). Neighbours blurred. | One card or pill per spoken word (0.2 to 0.5 s); the row slides left; active label bright. | Rapid lists ("scenarios, case studies, hypotheticals"). Keeps pace with the voice. (03, 11, 16) |
| 19 | **Notebook diagram** | Light cream ground with a thin grid. Black-filled pills for mind-map nodes; outlined centre node; thin black connecting lines. Calendar grid; red hand-drawn ellipse to circle one item. | Nodes pop in one per spoken word; lines draw from the centre; the red circle draws on. | Planning, brainstorming, calendars. Feels like a real working session. (04) |
| 20 | **Full-screen B-roll burst** | Real footage full frame (macro cards on felt, movie clips, phone in hand, cinematic desk shot). Caption stays. | Cut on every word, 0.2 to 0.3 s each; or one 1 to 3 s clip. | A vivid example the voice names ("casino", "mystery movies"). Pattern break from the graphic panel. (02, 10, 20) |
| 21 | **Full A-roll beat** | Talking head full screen, punched in chest-up. One-word white bold caption at about y 1140 to 1190. | Hard cut in and out. 0.4 to 5 s. | About 28% of screen time. Used on emotional or transition words ("junk", "immediately", "never"), opinions, and "snap back" lines between sections. Resets the eye and builds trust in the person. (all) |
| 22 | **CTA lead-magnet card** | Title 'Comment *"Word"*' (keyword in quotes, italic serif) over line 2 in the red box ("To Get The Doc"). Under it: a YouTube thumbnail card + channel row, or a grid of white doc pages, or a Claude skill icon. Light theme in most reels. | Title builds; the card scales in; the camera pushes into the thumbnail then pulls out to the channel row. | The last 5 to 12 s. Shows the exact thing they get, so the comment feels worth it. Final shot: full A-roll with a stacked caption 'Comment / *"word"* / send'. (all) |
