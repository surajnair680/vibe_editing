Why: he rejected v2 as "just a bunch of text, no visual element". Every line now shows something.

Look: dark Vox collage. Black and deep red grounds with heavy grain (brand MOTION.md). Everything else is paper:
torn bone / cream cut-outs with a white fibre edge and shadow, halftone, red marker circles and crosses, title-red
highlighter, stamps. Motion stepped on twos (12 poses a second). Real photos and his clips stay unfiltered, pinned
as torn prints. Assets: C = Codex image (objects only, no drawn faces), K = made in code, H = his photo or clip.

Layout: his talking cut-out sits in the lower part of the frame, the visual for each line sits above him
(y 300 to 1050), a small caption strip runs at the top. Big moments go full frame.
