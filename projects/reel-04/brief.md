# reel-04 brief (final = v3, 2026-11-01)

- **What:** a cinematic edit of an ordinary work session (Suraj at his desk), made to learn cinematic editing with AI.
  Private, not for posting (no blurring of the client dashboard needed). 9:16, 30 fps, 32 s, no voice.
- **Feel:** the viewer is inside his world. Moody, focused, quiet. Structure from his notes on the five-shot system
  (establishing, action with foreground, medium, close-up, over the shoulder) and multiple angles of one action.
- **Structure (timeline.py, fitted to the 92.3 BPM track):** already at work 0-10.37 (wide, door frame, behind the
  chair; snap pull-out at the open) / flow montage on the beat 10.37-18.17 / the breath 18.17-23.37 (music muffled) /
  exit 23.37-28.57 (stand, walk into the lens) / empty chair, REC iris closes to black 28.57-31.97.
- **Look:** moody warm grade (teal shadows, warm highlights, lifted blacks), halation, vignette, grain, digital
  camera moves on every shot (work/build.py).
- **Music:** ElevenLabs "moody minimal electronic", assets/music/m3_moody.mp3 (two other options kept).
- **SFX:** 9 ElevenLabs sounds (assets/sfx), risers softened (v2). Cue list work/sfx_cues.json.
- **Overlays (v3):** mini vlog pack V4 corners + "the quiet hours", "locked in.", "the world goes quiet",
  V12 REC iris + "same time tomorrow." (work/overlays.py).
