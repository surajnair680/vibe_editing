# reel-08 treatment: "Comeback Day 1" in Urban Sketch · Pen & Wash

## 1. Three structures

A. **Diary of pages.** Each line is its own sketchbook page, turned by her hand. Clear, but five page turns in 36 s
   feel like slides, and the story has no visual turn.
B. **The colour that left and came back.** One tall sketchbook page that the camera travels down, top to bottom, like
   scrolling her life. The good old days are in colour; "life happened" draws in ink only, and the gym (a coral
   dumbbell, the one coloured thing) slowly lifts off the page. "Starting again" brings colour back, a little at a time,
   and by the seawall the page is full colour. Ends on a page turn to a blank page that says "Day 2".
C. **One street corner, four times.** The same view of her block at four moments (busy, rain, gym, seawall). Elegant,
   but the gym and the seawall are different places, so it fights the script.

**Chosen: B.** Colour is the story: it shows the habit fading and coming back without any extra words, and the
page turn at the end is the series' promise (every episode is a new page).

## 2. Logline and arc

A woman who fell off the gym starts again on Day 1 and invites you to watch every step.
Setup (her, the mirror, she used to train) → turn (life drains the colour, the gym disappears; today she starts and
her body remembers nothing) → ending (colour floods back on the seawall, she invites you, the page turns to Day 2).

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from** urban sketchers' travel journals (Instagram sketchbook flip-throughs): line first, wash second,
  handwritten date and place, colour off the line. From *Paperman*: one coloured object in a monochrome world.
- **Don't take:** any drawing, layout, character design or type from them. Nothing from the library demo.

## 5. Beat sheet (film seconds)

- 0.0 page already being drawn, her at the mirror; "COMEBACK DAY 1" writes on top. Corner note "day 1 · Vancouver · rain".
- 0.5 "I used to work out five days a week." A small week tracker in the margin, M T W T F ticked in coral.
- 2.6 "Then life happened." Tracker ticks smear grey. Camera starts down the page.
- 4.1 to 10.1 "Final year of uni" (books, laptop, cap) · "moved countries" (suitcase, plane line) · "started working
  part-time" (café apron, cup) · rain over the city. Ink only, grey rain wash.
- 7.9 to 10.1 "and the gym just slowly disappeared": coral dumbbell lifts away, line by line.
- 10.1 to 11.1 near silence (rain only).
- 11.2 "So today, I'm starting again." Whip-tilt to the gym floor, pen draws her hands on knees, colour blooms on her.
- 13.8 "And my body remembers..." breath curls, sweat dabs. 16.1 "nothing." punch-in, small shake, music stops.
- 17.6 to 26.8 seawall: crane up, she walks, colour washes in behind her. Margin checklist writes on:
  "every step" · "from day 1" · crossed out "extreme diets" · crossed out "5am punishment".
- 27.9 "If you've fallen off too, come do this with me." Push-in, she waves.
- 31.9 "See you on Day 2." Page turn at 33.0, blank page, pen writes "Day 2". Hold to 36.0.

## 6. Cue map

Solo nylon-style guitar (sampler) + soft brushes + upright bass, 84 BPM in 4/4 (one bar = 2.857 s).
- Bars 1 to 2 (0 to 5.7): guitar alone, warm, major.
- Bars 2 to 4 (5.7 to 10.1): thinner, minor colour, a little faster strumming as life rushes; stops at 10.1.
- 10.1 to 11.2: silence (rain only). First sound after it: the pen stroke of her on the gym floor.
- 11.2 to 16.1: guitar comes back softly, picking; stops dead on "nothing" (16.1) for the gag, 0.8 s silence.
- 17.2 to 33.0: full: guitar, bass, brushes, a light glockenspiel line when colour arrives; drops to guitar alone on
  "See you on Day 2". Last chord rings into the page turn.
Every cut and key action is checked against these times by script (0 ms target).

- Corner note and checklist in Reenie Beanie, sepia ink.
