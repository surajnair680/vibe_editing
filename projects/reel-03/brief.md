# Brief: reel-03

A shot-for-shot remake of a reference edit (`raw/reference.mp4`, notes in `refs/`) with the user's own footage.
Learning exercise. The user credits the original creator in the Instagram caption.

## 1. The job
- **Video type:** moody "edit" style mindset reel (vfx-heavy, lo-fi night look)
- **One-line goal:** the viewer feels that results come from wanting it as badly as breathing, and sees the coach as someone who lived it.
- **Audience:** the user's Instagram followers: people who want to build their best physique (training, muscle, fat loss, mindset).
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels
- **Format:** 9:16 (1080x1920). Frame rate to match the edit's cut timing (reference is 25 fps).
- **Length:** same as the reference, 22.2 s.

## 2. Message
- **Voice:** Tom Platz, taken unchanged from the reference.
- **Audio:** the reference's full audio (voice + music) used exactly as is. No changes, no new music, no isolation.
- **Hook:** black screen, tiny words, the silence, then "breathe." lands on the first image.
- **Call to action:** none. Pure mindset.
- **Must-show:** the user's face and physique (their own footage replaces every shot).

## 3. Vibe
- **Everything matches the reference:** cut timing, pacing (slow, flurry, slow, stillness), caption style
  (tiny lowercase centred words, big coloured "breathe." and "okay?"), looks (night, grain, glow, chromatic
  aberration, glitch textures, light rays, vignettes, warm film burns), black open and black close.
- **Only change:** the footage.
