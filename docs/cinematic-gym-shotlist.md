# Cinematic gym shot list (reference)

Source: "Cinematic Workout Structure" by Matt Nikishin (@mattnikishin.mp4), a free resource the user shared on
2026-10-30. The exact shot order for a full gym session: 9 steps, 35 shots, 3 shot types. Film all of it once and
you have a B-roll library for months.

## When to use this
- **Footage requests:** when a video needs gym or physique B-roll the user hasn't filmed yet, pick shots from this list
  by name and step number (e.g. "Step 03 shot 3: hands picking up the bar, close up"). Still describe the feel of each
  moment (lessons-learned section 3 and the reel-03 rule), but this list gives the user a shared vocabulary.
- **Planning a gym session shoot:** if the user asks what to film, hand them this list in order, with the cinematic tips.
- **Sorting a footage dump:** tag each clip the user sends with its step, shot and type (LS / MS / CU), so the beat
  sheet can say which shot covers which line.
- **Cutting gym B-roll:** a sequence reads well when it moves LS (set the scene) to MS (the effort) to CU (the texture),
  then back out. The list already alternates them within each step.

## Shot types
| Tag | Name | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| LS | Long shot | Whole body and the space around. Sets the scene. |
| MS | Medium shot | Waist up. Shows the movement and the effort. |
| CU | Close up | Hands, feet, veins, details. The texture shots. |

## The shot order

### 01 Walking in
1. Follow behind walk in (MS)
2. Entering the gym (LS)

### 02 Jump rope + short run
Jump rope 1 to 2 min. Run 5 to 10 min with 1 min sprint intervals. Only record the sprints.
1. Jump rope: front (LS)
2. Jump rope: side profile (MS)
3. Jump rope: tracking (MS)
4. Run: front (LS)
5. Run: side profile (MS)
6. Pressing buttons (CU)
7. Feet hitting treadmill (CU)
8. Gassed (MS)
9. 180 walk (LS/MS)

### 03 Power cleans
Light to moderate weight. 4x3 explosive reps.
1. Low angle track up (LS)
2. Front clean (MS)
3. Hands picking up the bar (CU)
4. Changing weights (CU)
5. Back clean (LS)

### 04 Squats
Moderate weight. 3x5 explosive reps.
1. Low angle rack (LS)
2. Side profile (MS)
3. Front tracking up and down (LS/MS)

### 05 Pull ups
No weight. 2x5.
1. Low angle tripod front, recording both people (LS)
2. Gripping the bar (CU)
3. Mirror shot (MS)

### 06 Bicep curl
3x5. Go heavy to get the veins.
1. Front (LS)
2. Side profile (MS)
3. Arms (CU)

### 07 Battle rope
3x30 s. Hard mode.
1. Front with ropes in foreground (MS)
2. 180 walk (LS/MS)
3. Gassed (MS)
4. Low angle (LS)

### 08 Stretch
1. Tripod shot showing both people (LS)
2. Walk out tracking (MS)

### 09 Flex
1. Mirror (LS)
2. Veins (CU)
3. Body only (MS)
4. Walking in, tripod (LS)

## Cinematic spice (3 tips)
1. **Always add foreground.** Shoot past a rack, rope or plate. Layers in front make the frame feel cinematic.
2. **Catch the light on low angles.** When you shoot low, get the light in frame for some flare.
3. **Shakiness is good.** It adds movement. Do not over-stabilise it.

## What this means for the edit
- Keep handheld shake in this kind of footage; don't stabilise it away by default. Ask before stabilising.
- Low angle shots with flare are hero shots: good places for a title, a drop or a slow-mo beat.
- "Gassed" and "180 walk" shots are natural breathers between hard sections and good for a voice line or caption.
- Flex step: "veins" (CU) and "body only" (MS) are physique shots. The reel-03 rule applies: at least 0.6 to 0.7 s of
  clear view before any effect takes over.
- Gyms are busy: check foreground layers and backgrounds for logos and bystanders (lessons-learned section 3).
